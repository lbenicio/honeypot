# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds
and levels of experience. Contributors are expected to help keep project
spaces respectful, constructive, and safe.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not on people.
- Respect other people's privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances.
- Trolling or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Publishing passwords, tokens, or sensitive honeypot data in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other project spaces, as well as when someone represents the project publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy, issue a
warning, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently, based on the
severity and pattern of behavior. They will handle reports as privately as
possible and explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the repository maintainer through the contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/lbenicio). If no private
contact method is available, open an issue asking for a private channel without
including sensitive details in public.
